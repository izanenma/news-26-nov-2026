One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026:
tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz as the threat spreads to the
Red Sea, Putin vowing retaliation as the war spills into Libya, a
Thanksgiving storm threatening 250 million Americans, markets sliding while
the Dow tops 50,000, Hungary's prime minister meeting Zelensky, and the Gaza
truce fraying.

`render.py` draws the front page (1024×1024 PNG) with PIL; `main.py` copies
`entry.png` to the output directory. Both runs write identical bytes.
